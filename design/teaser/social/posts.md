## LinkedIn

Media: teaser.mp4, thumbnail: thumbnail.png

I redesigned my blog.

It had turned into a wall of text, and the charts looked like every other dashboard. I wanted it to look more like my notebook. So now it's paper, a marker and a pen.

What changed:
- I removed 7 chart and diagram packages. Every figure is now a small component that returns plain SVG.
- One SVG filter gives every shape the rough marker edge. I didn't draw a single rough shape by hand.
- Every post has its own line drawing in the header, and it moves.
- Each post gets a colour from its tags, and that colour follows it everywhere.

I wrote up how it's built, filter code included:
https://saybackend.com/blog/saybackend-redesign-paper-marker-pen
